Gender

A five-stage pipeline — detect, gate for riders, gate for quality, score with two models, calibrate against field observation — turning street-level imagery into the inputs behind the Gender Presence Index. See Methods for the full architecture; this page covers how well it performs and what it gets wrong.

Why two models, not one

EfficientNet-B3 and YOLO11m-cls are trained on the same crops but trusted for different things. EfficientNet drives every continuous gender score in the released data — YOLO alone is barely above chance for female-vs-male (ROC-AUC ≈ 0.535)[cite: 4]. YOLO drives the child gate instead, since EfficientNet's child head fires too broadly[cite: 4]. The two only get averaged for the discrete label decision, where their independent error patterns partially cancel right at the threshold[cite: 4]. Note that while the training author explicitly warned against using a pseudo-softmax average for these scores, the deployed pipeline utilizes this ensemble method regardless[cite: 3].

The confusion matrices below come from a manual audit sweeping detection confidence from 0.02 to 0.09 — below the pipeline's actual 0.22 operating threshold[cite: 4]. Read as a sensitivity check: they show what classification quality looks like if the detector were run far more permissively than it actually is, and how much "Noise" (non-person false positives) contaminates the sample at very low thresholds[cite: 4].

Filtering before scoring

The detector is highly sensitive, locating 188% of everyone the human annotators counted[cite: 1]. However, the gender classification model only evaluates 12% to 22% of these detected individuals[cite: 1, 3]. Roughly 65% of raw person detections are motorcycle or bicycle riders — filtered by a dual gate (geometric IoU against vehicle boxes, plus a learned EfficientNet-B0 classifier) before any gender scoring happens[cite: 4]. The rider gate alone removes two-thirds of all detected people, and it has never been validated against hand labels[cite: 1, 3]. Validation, Prior Shift, and Thresholds

Training vs. Reality (Prior Shift): The model was deliberately trained on a dataset heavily weighted toward women (49.6% female) to maximize minority class recall, whereas Jakarta's actual street population is roughly 25.4% female[cite: 3]. The deployment threshold is used specifically as a counterweight to offset this designed training bias[cite: 3].

Aggregate Validation: At the deployed threshold (currently noted in documentation as 0.6275), the model reports a male-to-female ratio of 2.85–2.97[cite: 1]. This closely matches the independent hand-labelled ground truth of 2.944, aligning to within about 2%[cite: 1]. This marks the first independent validation of the threshold's aggregate accuracy[cite: 1].

Per-Person Accuracy Limitations: While the aggregate ratio is accurate, it is achieved because opposing errors cancel each other out, not because individual classifications are reliable[cite: 1]. For women, the model's sensitivity is 0.16–0.24, with a Cohen's κ of 0.14–0.19[cite: 1]. The system finds approximately 53% of the women that annotators recorded, and when it flags a detection as a woman, it is incorrect 42.5% of the time[cite: 1]. Furthermore, the "Male" category functions as a residual class; 38% of "male" classifications on audited crops are not adult men[cite: 1, 3].

Validating the child gate

A manual audit of 8,321 classifier crops across all models, phases, and confidence bins checked the child gate specifically for adult contamination[cite: 4]. Weighting audit rates by where child predictions actually concentrate (31% sit in the 0.6 confidence bin, only 19% in the 0.9 bin), the expected true composition of the 12,344 child-labelled rows is approximately 58% genuine children, 26% adult males, 9% adult females, and 7% riders or noise[cite: 4].

Propagated to the overall gender counts, misrouted adult males amount to ~3.5% of the total male count, and misrouted adult females ~5.0% of the total female count[cite: 4]. These largely offset each other in the male:female ratio, and the net direction is conservative — the correction slightly increases the reported female share rather than inflating it[cite: 4].

Data integrity

Two bugs were found and repaired in this pipeline: a missing per-box index that scrambled score-to-person assignment in multi-person images, and a filename-collision bug that cropped roughly 150,000 images from the wrong source in one collection phase[cite: 4]. Crucially, the box_idx bug corrupts multi-person scenes, which is not gender-neutral — hand labels show that women appear in mixed-gender groups 23% of the time, compared to 9% for men[cite: 3]. Post-repair, an apparent demographic difference that had appeared in one phase vanished entirely — all four phases now agree on the naive male:female ratio to within 0.02[cite: 4].
