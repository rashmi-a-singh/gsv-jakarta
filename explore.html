Cell color blends the active phases' brand colors, weighted by each phase's panorama count in that cell; opacity scales with total density (cells hold 0–7 panoramas each, consistent with the ~3.5 average panoramas per hexagon across the sampling grid). An earlier per-point version of this map covered a subset of panoramas and struggled past a few hundred thousand individual markers — aggregating to the actual 68,685 sampling cells, rather than plotting all 242,439 panoramas individually, is what makes the full dataset renderable in a browser at all.
